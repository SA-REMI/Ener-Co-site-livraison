Management policy

Quality, prevention, responsibility and continuous improvement.

Ener-Co's management frames its commitments within a logic of quality, HSE prevention, operational control, environmental responsibility and continuous improvement, tailored to the reality of our interventions on sensitive technical and industrial sites.

In brief

In the course of its industrial energy distribution activities, Ener-Co structures its operations around clear requirements: personal safety, quality of execution, regulatory compliance, customer satisfaction, skills development and performance monitoring.

This approach is built on principles consistent with the requirements of structured quality and HSE prevention management, tailored to the reality of our interventions on sensitive technical and industrial sites.

Commitment of the management.

The management defines, supports and regularly reviews the company's commitments to ensure consistency between the objectives set, the resources deployed and the expected results.

01

Ensure the safety and preserve the health of employees, contractors and third parties.

02

02 · Quality

Quality of services and customer satisfaction.

Ener-Co commits to delivering services that comply with client requirements, applicable standards, the technical constraints of each site and the company's internal rules.

This requirement relies on preparation, interface control, resource matching and execution oversight throughout the project.

Customer feedback and field observations are integrated into the process to fuel continuous improvement of practices.

  • Clarification of the need and scope of intervention.
  • Preparation of operations ahead of on-site work.
  • Control of technical and organisational interfaces.
  • Matching resources to the actual work carried out.
  • Execution oversight and verification of sensitive points.
  • Integration of customer feedback and field observations.
Customer satisfaction is an essential performance indicator of the company and drives the continuous improvement of its practices.
03 · Health & safety

Risk prevention and control.

Risk prevention is an absolute priority, given the nature of the company's activities, especially on technical, industrial and sensitive environments.

01

Identification, assessment and continuous updating of risks.

02

Preparation of interventions and prior analysis of working conditions.

03

Prevention plans, PPSPS and documents applicable to each site.

04

Lockout/tagout procedures, voltage absence verification and intervention securing.

05

Monitoring of authorisations, permits and mandatory training.

06

Organisation of safety briefings and pre-task stand-ups.

07

Analysis of incidents, dangerous situations and near-misses.

08

Deployment of suitable corrective and preventive actions.

The company also ensures that organisational and human factors are considered when analysing work situations, in order to strengthen real risk control on site.
04 · Technical

Control of technical interventions.

Ener-Co's interventions, MV, LV switchboards, distribution panels, busways and power connections, are carried out within a demanding technical control framework, supported by preparation, field experience and systematic feedback.

  • Define intervention methods suited to each site context.
  • Verify the adequacy of equipment, tooling and lifting or handling means.
  • Control work at height, handling and interfaces with existing installations.
  • Account for the specific requirements of live or regulated sites.
  • Secure connection, testing and energisation phases.
  • Deliver readable, reliable execution aligned with client expectations.

Each project is the subject of dedicated preparation: context analysis, method definition, resource verification, interface validation with existing installations.

Critical phases, connection, testing and energisation, are conducted within a formalised framework, in direct coordination with the site's operations teams.

05 · Teams

Skills, working conditions and commitment.

Ener-Co considers that the lasting quality of services depends directly on the skills, working conditions and level of commitment of the teams.

Work organisation is adapted to the real constraints of each site, sharing of practices is encouraged and field feedback is actively supported.

Every intervention is an opportunity to grow collective know-how: formalised feedback, tracked authorisations, guided skills growth.

  • Keep the required authorisations and qualifications up to date.
  • Organise regulatory and useful technical training.
  • Develop the know-how tied to the trades of energy distribution.
  • Foster the transmission of practices and feedback.
  • Limit physically demanding situations whenever possible.
  • Encourage staff participation in improving practices.
Every team member is an actor of safety, quality and collective progress.
06 + 07 · Responsibilities

Environment & ethical conduct.

Two parallel pillars of Ener-Co's approach: the control of environmental impacts and professional loyalty in relationships with clients, suppliers and third parties.

06 · Environment

Environmental responsibility.

Aware of the impacts of its activities, Ener-Co integrates the environmental dimension into the preparation, execution and monitoring of its interventions.

  • Prevent pollution and limit the environmental impacts of activities.
  • Sort site waste and direct it to appropriate channels.
  • Control the use, storage and handling of hazardous products.
  • Reduce the impact of travel through adapted organisation.
  • Monitor relevant indicators (travel, consumption).
  • Raise team awareness of good environmental practices.
07 · Ethics & compliance

Professional conduct and responsible purchasing.

Ener-Co conducts its activities in compliance with applicable regulations, contractual requirements and the principles of professional loyalty.

  • Comply with the regulatory framework applicable to activities.
  • Keep a watch on requirements relevant to the company.
  • Adopt ethical conduct in commercial and operational relationships.
  • Protect the confidentiality of information, particularly on sensitive sites.
  • Factor compliance and reliability criteria into supplier selection.
  • Promote clear, responsible and lasting working relationships.
