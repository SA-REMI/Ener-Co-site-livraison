Published project · Steel industry / KiloAmps

Copper tube replacement · steelworks site

Summer intervention at a major French steelworks: replacement of copper tube sets by a team of six Ener-Co employees, within a tight shutdown window. Weekend work, anchoring on concrete structures and handling of bulky parts in a confined space, with a constant focus on safety and quality of execution.

MV substation installation · Panattoni

MV substation · Panattoni, Colombier-Saugnieu.

Installation of an MV substation on the Panattoni site in Colombier-Saugnieu: equipment setup, preparation of technical routings, organisation of connections and careful treatment of interfaces in a dedicated technical room. The project highlights clean execution, a clear reading of the installation and rigorous integration of power and auxiliary links.

Site
Panattoni · Colombier-Saugnieu
Scope
MV substation layout, routings, connections and interfaces
Context
Dedicated technical room · integration and readability of equipment
Reading
Quality of installation, clarity of routings and attention to detail
