Six expertises

Six integrated technical expertises.

Each link of power distribution is handled in-house, without subcontracting. From MV preparation to energisation, the same continuity of responsibility covers the entire project.

01 / MV

Medium voltage

Preparation of intervention sequences, interface management and organisation of works on structured electrical infrastructure.

02 / LV

LV feeders

Cable pulling, line adaptation, power connection and interface with low-voltage main distribution boards.

03 / Switchboards

Switchboards & outlets

Switchboard supply, tie-ins to existing equipment, management of site constraints and operational continuity.

04 / Busways

Busways

Layout, supporting, junctions, line replacement and adaptation of rigid networks on technical sites.

05 / Power

Power connections

Interfaces with transformers, LV boards, existing switchboards and industrial process or utility equipment.

06 / Energisation

Energisation

Final checks, end-of-project coordination and return to service according to the assigned scope.

Approach

A field company, structured for demanding projects.

Ener-Co operates without subcontracting on operations requiring rigour, coordination and adaptability. The challenge is not limited to pulling a line: it means connecting, integrating, securing and returning to service in environments where execution quality conditions operations.

01
No subcontracting, direct responsibility

Direct execution, site coordination and interface management, with no diluted responsibility or cascading subcontracting.

02
Sensitive sites & live processes

Data centres, heavy industry, critical infrastructure, utilities and regulated technical environments. Interventions on operational sites.

03
Structured HSE framework

MASE Rhône-Alpes certification, proven procedures and methods adapted to sensitive and regulated environments.

04
Clear client reading

A single point of contact, a clear scope, a measurable commitment. A credible answer for industrial principals and large groups.
