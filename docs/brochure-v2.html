Who we are

Ener-Co covers the entire industrial power distribution chain: MV preparation, LV feeders, switchboards and outlets, busways, power connections and energisation. We do not outsource site execution. No cascade of subcontractors, no diluted responsibility: one accountable interlocutor, from the first MV cable to final energisation.
